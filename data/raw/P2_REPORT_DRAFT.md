# Data and estimation

The NIFTY 50 price index uses 4,671 official NSE Indices daily closes from 1 January 2008 through 9 November 2026. HDFC Bank, Infosys and Hindustan Unilever use Yahoo dividend- and split-adjusted closes. Comparing Yahoo with NSE recovered 46 index observations and identified six exchange closures with repeated Yahoo stock closes. We removed those rows. NIFTY excludes ordinary dividends, and the three stock series each lack 28 sessions in the official index calendar. We use 252 observations per year, so the stock estimates approximate some multi-session moves as daily returns.

The risk-free proxy uses the latest 91-day Government of India T-bill auction yield available by each last NSE session of the month. We treat the quote as available one calendar day after the auction and convert the simple ACT/365 quote to a continuously compounded rate. The mean of the 226 monthly observations from January 2008 to October 2026 was 6.30% annually. The rolling estimates use the rate available for each month.

NIFTY's estimated annual drift was 9.62% (approximate 95% GBM confidence interval 0.43% to 18.81%) and volatility was 20.19%, with an approximate standard error of 0.21 percentage points. At risk aversion 3, the full-sample unconstrained Merton fraction was 27.12%. These standard errors assume constant-parameter GBM. The wide drift interval illustrates the uncertainty in using historical returns to estimate the model input. Sample skewness was −0.33 and excess kurtosis was 16.07. The Jarque–Bera statistic was 50,247.65, with a p-value below numerical reporting precision, indicating substantial departures from normality.

The official NIFTY history has an average of 247.72 sessions per complete year from 2008 through 2025. The plan uses 252 sessions annually. Using 248 instead, on the same returns and with the same risk-free rate, changes annual drift from 9.62% to 9.47%, volatility from 20.19% to 20.03%, and the gamma-3 stock fraction from 27.12% to 26.29%. This sensitivity preserves the qualitative observation of substantial drift uncertainty. It has not been evaluated in the later backtest. The ACT/365 T-bill conversion raises the average rate by 0.154 percentage points relative to the plan's conversion on the same quotes.

Sources and verification details are recorded in data/raw/P2_README.md. See `results/p2_params.csv` for estimates for all four assets and `figures/p2_rolling_^NSEI.png` for the rolling NIFTY estimates.
